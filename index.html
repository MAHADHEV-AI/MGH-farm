The Komban Recipe Corner

How to cook traditional South Indian Country Chicken (Naatu Kozhi) to perfection.

Authentic Chettinad Naatu Kozhi Kozhambu

Because Country Chicken has firmer, natural muscle fibers, it requires a slow simmer or 4-5 pressure cooker whistles to release its rich bone marrow broth.

  • Step 1: Marinate 1kg Naatu Kozhi with 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, and rock salt for 20 mins.
  • Step 2: In cold-pressed sesame oil, sauté shallots, fresh curry leaves, and freshly ground whole spices (coriander seeds, black pepper, fennel, and dried red chilies).
  • Step 3: Sear the meat on high heat for 5 minutes to seal in the flavors, then add water and pressure cook for 4 whistles until the meat is fork-tender.
